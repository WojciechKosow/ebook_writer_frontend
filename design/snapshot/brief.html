Brief — the workspace

Everything now uses the Ink & Paper look. The public site has faint drifting pages and letters in the background; the signed-in workspace (Workspace and Dark mode rows) has none and feels a little flat without them.

Propose how ambient paper could live in the workspace: it is a place people work and read for long stretches, so it must stay calm and out of the way of content — not the landing's effect copied over. Consider where it appears (sidebar, empty states, page headers, the writing view), how it moves (if at all), how it reacts to state (a book being written, a finished book) and how it behaves in dark mode and with reduced motion.
